MindshrBot

MindshrBot is the web crawler for Mindshr, a data service that counts public mentions of companies and products in the news.

What it does

It fetches publicly available news pages to count which companies and products they mention.

How it behaves

  • It obeys robots.txt, including Crawl-delay.
  • It makes no more than one request every few seconds to any site.
  • It does not log in and does not go behind paywalls.
  • It identifies itself in every request with this user agent:
MindshrBot/0.1 (+https://mindshr.ai/bot; bot@mindshr.ai)

What we do with page content

  • We read the page to count which companies and products it mentions.
  • We keep a private copy so we can re-check those counts.
  • We never republish, sell or share article text. The only thing we publish is numbers: how many articles mentioned something on a given day.
  • We use automated tools, including AI models, to work out which company a mention refers to.
  • We do not use page content to train generative AI models.

How to block it

Add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: MindshrBot
Disallow: /

Contact

bot@mindshr.ai. We respond to requests to slow down or stop.
