MindshrBot

MindshrBot is the web crawler for Mindshr. Mindshr gathers public facts and figures from across the web and organises them into one consistent, queryable dataset, so that people and the applications they build can answer questions with real numbers and see where each number came from.

What we do with a page

  • We read it for the facts and figures on it, and record them with the page's address, title and date.
  • Every figure stays tied to its source. When Mindshr gives an answer, it names the page the figure came from and links to it.
  • We never republish, sell or share the content of pages or articles. A private copy is kept only so we can recheck and correct our data.
  • We do not use page content to train generative AI models.
  • We use automated tools, including AI models, only to read figures off a page and to work out what they refer to.

How the crawler behaves

  • It obeys robots.txt, including Crawl-delay, under the name MindshrBot.
  • It makes at most one request every five seconds to a site, and backs off when asked (a 429 response or a Retry-After header).
  • It does not log in and does not go behind paywalls. On paywalled sites it reads headlines from public feeds only.

How to recognise it

It identifies itself in every request with this user agent:

MindshrBot/0.1 (+https://mindshr.ai/bot; bot@mindshr.ai)

How to control it

To stop it entirely, add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: MindshrBot
Disallow: /

You can also disallow part of your site or set a Crawl-delay. Changes take effect within 24 hours.

Contact

bot@mindshr.ai. Write to us to slow the crawler down, to stop it, or to let it in if your site currently blocks it. We respond to every request.
